The text_indentation function separates sentences at periods, question marks,
and colons with two newline characters.

>>> text_indentation = __import__("5-text_indentation").text_indentation
>>> text_indentation("Hello. World? Yes: done")
Hello.
<BLANKLINE>
World?
<BLANKLINE>
Yes:
<BLANKLINE>
done

>>> text_indentation("  alpha  \n beta?   gamma:  ")
alpha
beta?
<BLANKLINE>
gamma:
<BLANKLINE>
